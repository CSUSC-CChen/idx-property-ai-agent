## Comparing the Markets

- **Most competitive:** Pasadena is the only one of the three selling above
  asking (103.2% sold-to-list). Irvine (98.0%) and San Diego (99.5%) both give
  buyers a small amount of negotiating room.
- **Fastest moving:** San Diego turns over quickest at 27 average days on
  market, versus 34 in Pasadena and 38 in Irvine.
- **Price tier:** Irvine has the highest median at $1,486,500, roughly 1.6x
  San Diego's $950,000. Pasadena sits between them at $1,298,000.
- **Price per square foot** tells a different story than median close price:
  Pasadena ($831) and Irvine ($812) are close, while San Diego ($726) is
  meaningfully cheaper per square foot. Median close price alone reflects the
  size of homes selling, not just how expensive the market is.
- **Average vs. median:** all three have averages above their medians (Irvine
  by ~$263K, San Diego by ~$232K, Pasadena by ~$362K), meaning high-value
  sales pull the average up in every market. Median is the more representative
  figure.
- **Thin first months:** March 2026 has 1–3 sales in every city because the
  window starts on 2026-03-31. Any month-over-month percentage anchored on
  that month should be read with care.
