## Comparing the Markets

These four markets span very different price tiers and dynamics:

- **Most competitive (seller's market):** San Francisco (107.7% sold-to-list)
  and Pasadena (103.0%) are both selling above asking on average, with San
  Francisco showing the fastest DOM (38 days) and the sharpest 6-month price
  climb (64.1%).
- **Most buyer leverage:** Beverly Hills (95.6% sold-to-list) and San Pedro
  (99.2%) are both selling at or below asking, with Beverly Hills carrying
  by far the longest average DOM (60 days) — typical of the luxury segment,
  where inventory sits longer.
- **Price tier spread:** Beverly Hills' median ($3,695,000) is roughly 4.6x
  Pasadena's, 4.4x San Francisco's, and 4.6x San Pedro's — a reminder that
  citywide medians should never be compared directly without accounting for
  the underlying property mix.
- **Average vs. median divergence:** Beverly Hills shows by far the largest
  gap between average ($6,819,611) and median ($3,695,000) close price,
  meaning a small number of very high-value sales are skewing the average
  upward. The other three markets have averages much closer to their
  medians, indicating a more typical, less skewed distribution of sales.
